## Platform

web

## Stack

Standalone, self-contained HTML page in `public/skycreation/` (same pattern as the V-Lights client page). GSAP + ScrollTrigger loaded from CDN. No build step.

## Users

Primary: homeowners in Navi Mumbai and Thane (Kharghar, Thane, Turbhe area) who have bought or are moving into a 2–3 BHK flat and want the whole home designed and executed by one team. They are comparing a few local designers, usually on phone, often via Instagram/WhatsApp, and want to trust that the designer will finish on time and on budget.

Secondary: small business owners (offices, CA firms, commercial fit-outs) — shown as supporting proof, not the lead.

## Product Purpose

Sky Creation is an interior design and execution studio founded and led by head designer Vivek Bhokare, operating for about 10 years. The site must turn a visiting homeowner into a consultation request (form, call, or WhatsApp).

## Positioning

Founder-led: the same designer (Vivek Bhokare) plans and oversees each project from consultation to installation. Design + execution under one roof, an 8-step process from site analysis to modular installation, and an exclusive 10-year warranty.

## Operating Context

Process (8 steps, from the existing site): Initial Consultation, Site Analysis, Concept Development, Space Planning, Design Development, Design Presentation (sketches + digital renderings), Final Renders, Implementation (materials, execution oversight, modular furniture and décor installation).

Services: 2D/3D design layout, civil space planning, residential interiors, master bathrooms, modular kitchens, office interiors.

Consultation form fields: first name, last name, email, contact number, project type (Commercial / Residential / Retail / Hospitality), message.
