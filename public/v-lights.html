Our Heritage

Lighting as a
Timeless Art

V-Lights was born from a single conviction: that a room's character lives in its light. Founded by master artisan Viktor Harlan, our atelier blends Old World craftsmanship with contemporary vision.

Each chandelier passes through dozens of hands — glassblowers, metalworkers, and crystal specialists — before finding its home in your space. We don't make lighting. We make heirlooms.
