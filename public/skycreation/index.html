Our process,
step by step

Every project follows the same eight steps, so you always know what happens next and nothing is built until you have seen it.

  1. 01

    Initial consultation

    We discuss your needs, budget and overall vision for the space.

  2. 02

    Site analysis

    We assess the layout, existing features, light and how the room needs to work.

  3. 03

    Concept development

    Colour schemes, material selections and furniture placement.

  4. 04

    Space planning

    A detailed layout plan that makes the most of every wall.

  5. 05

    Design development

    Specific materials, finishes and lighting are chosen.

  6. 06

    Design presentation

    You see the full plan in sketches and digital renderings.

  7. 07

    Final renders

    We finalise the renders with you until every room is right.

  8. 08

    Implementation

    We order materials, oversee execution and install the furniture and décor.
